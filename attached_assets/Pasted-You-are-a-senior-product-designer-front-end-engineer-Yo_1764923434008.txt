2. Visual Design Direction

Overall style: Clean, minimal, lots of white space, clear hierarchy.

Default theme: Light theme with a soft gray background and white cards. Also prepare a dark mode variant (even if just via simple theme classes / variables).

Primary accent color: A rich blue or teal (for buttons, highlights).

Secondary accents: Green for profitable trades, red for losing trades.

Typography:

Large, bold page titles

Medium-weight section titles

Comfortable line height for tables & notes

Spacing: Generous padding (16–24px) inside cards, consistent gaps between sections.
